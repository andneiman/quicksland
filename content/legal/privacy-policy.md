## 1. Scope

Quicks is an AI-powered workspace for recording and transcribing audio, creating and organising content, generating materials and applications, collaborating, and sharing through private or public links.

This Policy applies to personal data processed by Purple through the Service. It does not apply to third-party websites, services, or applications that have their own privacy policies.

If a school, employer, or other organisation provides your access to Quicks, that organisation may be the controller of content processed through its workspace and Purple may process that content on its instructions. Please contact the organisation for information about its own privacy practices.

## 2. Data we collect

Depending on how you use the Service, we may collect:

### Account and contact data

Your name, email address, account identifiers, login information, organisation, preferences, and information you provide when contacting support.

### User content

Prompts, documents, notes, boards, transcripts, files, generated materials, applications, websites, comments, sharing settings, and other content you create, upload, store, or share through the Service.

### Audio and transcripts

Audio captured when you activate a recording or transcription feature is processed temporarily to create a transcript. **Purple does not store or retain the original audio recording.**

Audio is used solely to generate a text transcript. Quicks does not create or retain a voiceprint or other biometric identifier from your audio, and does not use audio for voice recognition, speaker identification, or any other biometric purpose.

We store the resulting transcript. Before storage, we apply automated measures designed to remove direct identifiers and de-identify the transcript. Automated de-identification may not remove every detail that could identify a person from context. A transcript may therefore remain personal data where a person can still be identified directly or indirectly.

### Usage and technical data

Purple does not currently retain device analytics or general usage analytics. Limited technical information may be processed temporarily where necessary to deliver a request, maintain a connection, or protect the security of the Service.

In the future, we may collect and retain information such as device type, operating system, browser, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, app version, language, time zone, activity logs, feature usage, crash reports, and diagnostic or security information. Before introducing material new collection, we will update this Policy and request consent where required by law.

### Collaboration and sharing data

Workspace membership, invitations, access permissions, public-link settings, and information about when shared content is accessed or changed.

### Payment data

If you purchase a paid plan, our payment provider may collect payment-card or other billing details. Purple generally receives transaction information such as the plan, amount, currency, payment status, and billing contact details, rather than full payment-card information.

### Cookies and similar technologies

Purple does not currently store cookies or use cookies for analytics or advertising. In the future, we may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies for authentication, preferences, security, analytics, and operation of the Service. Before introducing such technologies, we will update this Policy and obtain consent where required by law.

## 4. Legal bases

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (**“GDPR”**) or similar law applies, we process personal data on one or more of the following legal bases:

- **Contract:** processing is necessary to provide the Service you requested and perform our Terms of Service.
- **Legitimate interests:** processing is necessary to secure, maintain, support, and understand the operation of the Service, prevent abuse, and protect our legal rights, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
- **Consent:** where you choose optional cookies, marketing, or another feature that legally requires consent. You may withdraw consent at any time.
- **Legal obligation:** processing is necessary to comply with applicable law, tax, accounting, regulatory, or lawful authority requirements.

Where you record or submit personal data about another person, you are responsible for having a valid legal basis and giving all notices required by law.

## 5. Recordings and other participants

The person who starts a recording controls when and whom to record. Before recording another person, that user must inform all participants and obtain any consent or permission required by applicable law. Where a participant is a minor, permission from a parent, guardian, school, or other authorised person may be required.

Purple processes audio only to provide the requested transcription or AI feature. Audio may pass temporarily through systems operated by Purple and our service providers, but Purple does not retain it after the required processing is completed.

If someone recorded you through Quicks and you have questions about that recording, you should first contact the person or organisation that made it. You may also contact us at [support@quicks.com](mailto:support@quicks.com).

These providers receive information needed to perform their services. Audio, prompts, transcripts, outputs, or other content may be transmitted to AI or transcription providers for processing.

Purple does not itself train AI models on user content. Third-party AI and technology services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, Purple is not responsible for processing carried out independently by a third-party provider acting as a separate controller.

We take reasonable steps to select and configure providers appropriately and to limit the data sent to them. You should not submit information that is confidential, highly sensitive, or unnecessary for your use of the Service. Information that has been fully anonymised and can no longer identify a person is not personal data under this Policy.

We may also disclose data:

- to a buyer, investor, successor, or affiliate as part of a merger, financing, reorganisation, sale, or transfer of all or part of our business;

Service providers may process personal data as our processors or, where applicable, as independent controllers under their own privacy terms. Their permitted purposes and obligations may differ depending on the provider and the Service feature involved.
